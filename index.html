政策叙事分析平台（NPF）NPF Policy Narrative Platform

方法论 × 工程Methods × engineering icgma/npf-policy-narrative-analysis

按 30 个二元叙事变量（角色、冲突范围、归因机制、情节与解决方案）编码政策文本，改编自 Shanahan, Jones & McBeth (2018) 发表的 NPF 方法论。核心是「LLM 辅助编码 + 专家标注 + 出版级信度统计」闭环。 Codes policy texts on 30 binary narrative variables — characters, scope of conflict, causal mechanisms, plot and solutions — adapted from the published NPF methodology of Shanahan, Jones & McBeth (2018). The core loop: LLM-assisted coding + expert annotation + publication-grade reliability statistics.

Python/Flask/LLM coding/Kappa · α · F1

我的工作横跨计算传播学、政策叙事分析与 AI 赋能的新闻教育，沿着「教学平台 — 研究工具 — 新闻实务」三条线索独立开发并开源成体系的工具链，坚持本地优先、隐私友好、可复现。icgma 是我在网上一致使用的名字——仓库、工具与这个站点，都归档在这同一个名字下。My work spans computational communication, policy narrative analysis, and AI-powered journalism education. Along three threads — teaching platforms, research tools, newsroom utilities — I design and maintain an open, reproducible toolchain: local-first, privacy-friendly, verifiable. icgma is the one name I use online; the repos, the tools and this site all live under it.

我习惯把研究方法做成可用的软件：为叙事政策框架（NPF）建编码与信度平台，为课堂建 RAG 教研中枢，为新闻现场建零上传的纯前端工具箱。好的传播学研究应当经得起信度检验，好的工具应当在没有网络的地方也能运行。I turn methods into software: a coding-and-reliability platform for the Narrative Policy Framework, a RAG teaching hub for the classroom, zero-upload browser tools for the field. Good communication research should survive a reliability check; good tools should run where there is no network.
