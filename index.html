我是一名计算社会科学研究者。我开发面向文本与内容的计算工具——LLM 辅助编码、信度分析、爬虫与可视化——并用它们研究政策叙事、媒体可信度与 AI 在新闻教育中的应用。 I am a computational social scientist. I build computational tools for text and content — LLM-assisted coding, reliability analysis, scraping and visualization — and use them to study policy narratives, media credibility, and AI in journalism education.

我目前在一所高校的新闻与传播学院任教，开设《AI 驱动的传媒内容制作》与《基础编程》等课程，并维护一套面向新闻实务的开源工具链——本地优先、隐私友好、可复现。icgma 是我在网上一致使用的名字，仓库、工具与本站都署名于此。 I currently teach at a university school of journalism and communication, offering courses on AI-driven media production and programming, and maintain an open-source toolchain for newsroom practice — local-first, privacy-friendly, reproducible. icgma is the one name I use online; the repos, the tools and this site all live under it.
